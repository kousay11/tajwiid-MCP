## Cross-checks done in tests

- `idhar_mutlaq` hits only words containing دني, بني, صنو or قنو: the four lexemes of book A p.6.
- Standard tanween marks occur exactly where the detector reports `idhar_halqi`; stacked marks everywhere else.
- Every `iqlab` hit carries the small high meem (283 of 283) and no other rule has one.
- The mim before ba or mim is written bare in this corpus; 496 and 832 positions are classified.
- 49 alef-lam words whose marks do not fit the article pattern are counted as skipped, not guessed.
- Closed lists named by Book A hit exactly the number of places the book names: Yajuj and Majuj (4),
  ruya, minsaa, saala (1 each), musada (2), the four fixed alefs (4), the two prophet exceptions (2),
  the 22 isolated letters of حي طهر (22), the 10 muthaqqal and 34 mukhaffaf isolated letters, the
  three lazim kalimi mukhaffaf (محياي and two آلآن), 24 closed ya'at and 6 opened ya'at, the 7 closed ya words of p.47, and the 11 occurrences of معي.
- A madd letter is never taken from a plain alef that is followed by a sukun or a shadda without a
  maddah: that alef is hamzat al-wasl (this removed over 1,000 false hits on the first draft).
- `hamza_two_words_same` and `hamza_two_words_different` have a rule record but no detector: the
  Qaloun text already carries the result of the rule and not the original hamzas.
- Every plural meem written bare before ba or meem carries two annotations on the same range, the
  sila (`madd_sila_sughra_meem`, variants iskan and sila) and ikhfa or idgham shafawi, noted as
  alternatives (1,314 places).
