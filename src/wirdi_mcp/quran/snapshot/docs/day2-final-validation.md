# Qaloun pagination: confirmation of the 559-page layout (public note)

This note replaces an internal audit of the Wirdi AI project and keeps its page-count findings.

| Question | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Is 559 the printed-page count of the King Fahd Complex Qaloun mushaf? | Yes. Printed page 559 (al-Ikhlas, al-Falaq, an-Nas) is PDF page 560; the PDF pages after it are lettered appendix pages |
| Why do other references show 573, 577, 604 or 612? | 573 and 577 are PDF page counts of two scans of the same 559-page layout, with different front and back matter. 604 is a different layout (digital text, Madinah style). |
| Is the page map correct? | The edges of every page were checked: the page counts match on all 557 numbered pages of a second, independent scan; all 1,114 first and last markers were verified (1,054 by a classifier, 60 by eye); all 114 surah starts are explained |
| Why do 459 page boundaries fall inside a verse? | In this edition the text flows continuously, 15 lines per page, and page breaks are not aligned to verse ends |
| Is any Quran text altered? | No. Every stored text is byte-identical to its source, and a checksum guards it |

The status `verified_against_edition` rests on this page-edge evidence. The scans used as evidence are
copyrighted and are not part of this repository.
